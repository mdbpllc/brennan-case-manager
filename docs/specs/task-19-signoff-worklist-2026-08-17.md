## §4 — Walk mechanics (the ruled shape, restated so the walk does not drift)

- **Michael verifies or declines each entry. The session records his word and nothing else.** It never nudges past a declined entry, and it never supplies a cite, upgrades a cite, or adopts wording.
- **Sequencing is EXECUTE-THEN-VERIFY** (ruled #95). All nine reworded entries were executed at #95/#96 before this walk, so no verification attaches to text that will not survive — the failure #93 named.
- **Verification attaches to WORDING.** For the nine entries carrying a `**Wording:** adopted` line, the look is against the **new** text; the old wording is quoted in the entry itself and in the drafting records, and is not what is being verified.
- **ROUTE-C is standing law** (ruled #95). If a flagged-not-adopted divergence turns out on close read to **change what the entry means**, conforming wording gets drafted and queued for his adopt/reject/edit — never adopted in the walk, never as a package, each entry put independently. A cite change implied by adopted wording is flagged and ruled as its own act.
- **Retrieval is not verification.** Every "RESOLVED" in the citator pass and workbook means *textually resolved in the named source*, never legally verified.
- **SOURCING binds every look.** TRCP text from the clean-authority PDF `Documents\Knowledge Repo\Civil\texas-rules-of-civil-procedure July 2026.pdf` (the §3 statute normalizer does **not** apply — different publisher, raw TRCP `AA` count 0). Case retrieval on FLP/CourtListener, throttled at 5 req/min. Each source named per item.
- **Output of the walk** is one packet: Status-line changes, a review-log-style record of what he verified and on what look, and queue closures.
