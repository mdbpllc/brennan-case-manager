### 2.3 `FOM-12` — the weekend rule, Michael's composite, stated once so it can be tested

His words, verbatim: ***"I am ruling with option 1, but with a caveat: If the deadline falls on the weekend and hence is due on the following business day, I want it to be made clear that the due date is the following business day, but also want strong encouragement that we satisfy and meet the deadline by the business day prior to the weekend/holiday."*** Re-asked narrower, two picks: **(a)** *"Per-obligation setting, confirmed at activation (Recommended)"* — how the module knows a given obligation rolls; **(b)** *"Weekends now; holidays flagged, not computed (Recommended)"*. Then, on the roll defaults: *"Ship as unknown; defaults come with the registry act (Recommended)"*.

**The rule (RULED / RULED-BY-SELECTION; the synthesis of the three states is Claude's reading, named in §11 limb 4 and confirmed there):**

1. Every obligation carries **`weekendRule ∈ { 'rolls-forward', 'no-roll', 'unknown' }`**, default `'unknown'` on every template at seed; set or confirmed by Michael at activation and editable after (logged, `FOD-6`).
2. For an occurrence, **R** = the rule date (`dueOnOverride ?? dueOn`). **If R is a Saturday or Sunday:** the **TARGET T** is the last business day before R (the Friday); the **DUE D** is: `rolls-forward` → the next business day after R (the Monday); `no-roll` → R; `unknown` → R, displayed with the `FOD-1` note. **If R is a weekday:** T = D = R. **Weekends only — no holiday is computed** (2.3(b)); a standing line on the register and the card says so (`FOD-24`).
3. **The lit moment, the lead window, the card horizon and the Outlook reminder all key off T.** `lightsOn = T − leadDays` (for `precision: month`, the earlier of that and the 1st — `FOM-6`).
4. **Display states, derived at render (nothing stored) — FIVE:** `pending` (today < lightsOn) · `lit` (lightsOn ≤ today ≤ T) · **`target-passed`** (T < today ≤ D — warning colour, the line reads *"target passed — due <D>"*) · **`overdue`** (today > D, with the day count from D) · **`past-date-unknown`** — under `unknown` with R on a weekend, from the day after T until done: the line reads *"past its date — a next-business-day rule may apply; not computed"* and the module NEVER prints "overdue" or a day count for that occurrence. **Precedence:** `past-date-unknown` takes precedence over `target-passed` and `overdue` wherever both would hold; `done` over everything. Under `rolls-forward` the row makes the roll plain — *"Due Mon Feb 1 (rolled from Sat Jan 30) · aim for Fri Jan 29"* (2027's calendar) — and under `no-roll` the row reads *"Due Sat Jan 30 · aim for Fri Jan 29"*; the encouragement line is the strong line in every state before T (`FOD-25`, TEXT ACT).
5. **The Outlook event sits on T** (DECISION 7); its body carries D and, where they differ, R and the roll.
