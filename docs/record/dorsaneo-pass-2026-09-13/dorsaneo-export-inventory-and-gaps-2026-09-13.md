Aggregating every such reference across the 4,617 documents gives 135 referenced chapters that ARE present and **at least 75 *Texas Litigation Guide* chapters that are referenced by title but have no document in the export** (a further ~40 referenced chapter numbers belong to other Matthew Bender titles by their subjects and are left out below).
